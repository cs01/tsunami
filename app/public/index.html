If the ground shakes near the ocean

  1. Drop, cover, hold on until the shaking stops.
  2. Walk uphill or away from the water right away. Don't wait for a siren. The shake is the warning.
  3. If the ocean suddenly pulls way out and you see the sea floor, a wave is coming. Go up high, fast.
  4. Stay up high for hours. The first wave is often not the biggest.
