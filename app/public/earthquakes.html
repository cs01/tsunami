What the shaking feels like

The number (magnitude) is how big the quake was. What you feel depends on how close you are. A medium quake right under you can shake harder than a giant one far away.

When the ground shakes

  1. Drop to your hands and knees, cover your head under a table, hold on until it stops.
  2. Stay inside. Don't run out while it's shaking. Things fall off buildings.
  3. Afterward, expect aftershocks. Smell gas? Get out and tell a grown-up.
  4. Near the ocean and it shook hard for a long time? Walk uphill now. Check for tsunamis.
